Mental Health 101
Behavioral health operations platform

Care for more people with the clinicians we already have.

Roughly 70% of people with a mental health condition receive no formal care — not because demand is missing, but because clinician time is drowned in paperwork, billing, no-shows, and mismatched referrals. Mental Health 101 puts AI agents around the clinician, never in place of one, so every hour of human care goes further.

Where clinician hours go today

Direct patient care~55%
Documentation & notes~25%
Billing, claims, admin~20%

Mental Health 101's target: return the gold share to care. Recovering even half of admin time adds the equivalent of ~1 clinical day per clinician per week.

Ten industry problems, one connected system

Each module below maps to a structural problem in the behavioral health market. Open a row to see how Mental Health 101 addresses it and which agent does the work.

The agent roster

Seven specialized AI agents run the operational layer of a practice. None of them deliver therapy. All of them report to humans.

Humans deliver care

Agents handle intake logistics, paperwork, and follow-up. Diagnosis, treatment, and therapeutic conversation belong to licensed clinicians, always.

Crisis goes to people, instantly

Any signal of crisis — in intake forms, messages, or check-ins — bypasses every queue and routes to an on-call human with full context. The agent's only job is speed.

Privacy is the product

Mental health data never trains models, never feeds advertising, and never leaves the care relationship. Every agent action is logged and auditable by the patient.

Evidence over engagement

Success is measured in validated outcomes (PHQ-9, GAD-7) and access metrics — not minutes in app. We don't optimize for screen time on a mental health product.
